About SUDZ

Owned & Operated By Silas Zeidler

SudZ Mobile Detailing is a mobile auto detailing business based in Danville, VA, serving Danville and the surrounding areas including Chatham, Ringgold, Blairs, and Gretna. We come to you — your driveway, your job, wherever your car is parked. No drop-off, no waiting at a shop.

SudZ is owned and operated by Silas Zeidler, a Danville local who started the business with one goal: do quality work and treat every customer right. From a quick exterior wash to a full interior restore on a heavily neglected vehicle, every job gets the same attention. We offer three packages — Express Wash, Refresh Detail, and Restore Detail — along with add-ons like wax and headlight restoration, and monthly fleet service for local businesses.
